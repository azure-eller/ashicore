## Overview

Ashicore is business operations software for inventory, manufacturing, sales, purchasing, planning, and related workflows. We collect and process information needed to provide the service, secure accounts, support customers, and operate the product.

## Data we process

Ashicore may process account details, organization details, team membership, operational records, uploaded files, support requests, device and log data, and integration data that customers choose to connect.

## How data is used

We use customer data to provide the service, maintain security, investigate issues, improve reliability, support integrations, and communicate about account or product matters.

## Support and deletion requests

For privacy questions or access requests, contact [support@ashicore.app](mailto:support@ashicore.app). To remove an account or associated personal data, follow the [data deletion instructions](/data-deletion).

## Updates

We may update this policy as the product, vendors, or legal requirements change. Material updates will be reflected on this page.
