## Contact

Email [support@ashicore.app](mailto:support@ashicore.app) for product support, security questions, account help, app review requests, or data deletion requests.

## Account access

Use [Open console](/console) to access the authenticated Ashicore console. If you are already signed in, Ashicore opens your workspace. If you were invited by a team, use the invitation link from your email.

## App and integration reviews

Vendor reviewers can use the public pages on this site for privacy, terms, security, subprocessors, and support contact details.

## Operational issues

Include the affected workflow, organization name, approximate time, and any visible request or error ID when reporting an issue.
