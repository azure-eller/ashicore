## Infrastructure and operations

Ashicore uses trusted service providers to host, store, deliver, observe, and support the product.

## Current subprocessors

- Vercel for application hosting and deployment.
- Neon for Postgres database infrastructure.
- Resend for transactional email delivery.
- Sentry for error monitoring and production diagnostics.
- Xero or QuickBooks only when a customer connects accounting workflows.

## Updates

This list may change as Ashicore adds or replaces infrastructure providers. Questions can be sent to [support@ashicore.app](mailto:support@ashicore.app).
