## User problem

Production is done, and Ashicore needs to show the finished stock that now exists.

## Steps

1. Open the manufacturing order.
2. Confirm material requirements and production status.
3. Record the actual finished quantity.
4. Complete the order.
5. Review the finished product lot.
6. Review the ledger for material and output movements.

## System behavior

Completion creates finished stock and closes out the production record according to the order state. The finished quantity should reflect reality, not just the plan.

## Edge cases

Short output, extra output, material substitutions, and failed batches should be recorded clearly. Avoid editing unrelated records to force the result to look clean.
