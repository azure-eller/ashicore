Ashicore runs alongside the systems you already use — it doesn't replace them. Connect your accounting provider so invoices and bills flow to the right place, pull online orders in from your storefront, and give planning assistants read-only access to your production data. Connections are managed under <span class="po-uikey">Settings → Integrations</span>. The Agent API has its own settings surface — <span class="po-uikey">Settings → Agent API</span> — separate from Integrations.

## What moves, and which way

A quick map of where data travels once a connection is live. Accounting is bidirectional; the storefront and agent surfaces are one-way.

| Connection | Direction | What moves |
|------------|-----------|------------|
| Xero / QuickBooks | <span class="po-tok po-tok--muted">out</span> | Sales invoices on shipment; supplier bills created from purchase orders. |
| Xero / QuickBooks | <span class="po-tok po-tok--muted">in</span> | Open purchase orders for receiving (both providers); customer and supplier contacts (Xero only). |
| Shopify | <span class="po-tok po-tok--muted">in</span> | Paid, unshipped storefront orders become sales orders. |
| Agent API | <span class="po-tok po-tok--muted">read-only</span> | Open sales orders, open manufacturing orders, product counts, lot counts, and BOM requirements (unit costs excluded). |

<Aside type="note" title="One accounting provider at a time">
Ashicore syncs accounting documents to exactly one of Xero *or* QuickBooks. To switch, disconnect one before connecting the other — existing records stay put either way.
</Aside>
