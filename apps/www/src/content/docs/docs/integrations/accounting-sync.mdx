Connect one accounting provider and Ashicore keeps your books in step with operations: sales invoices and supplier bills flow out as documents post, and open purchase orders and contacts flow in. Ashicore never keeps its own ledger — your accounting system stays the book of record.

<p class="po-meta">
  <span class="po-chip">Provider: <b>Xero or QuickBooks</b></span>
  <span class="po-chip">Settings → Integrations</span>
</p>

<Aside type="note" title="Exactly one provider, connected via OAuth">
Connect **Xero** or **QuickBooks** — not both. Xero can authorize more than one organisation — you pick the active one and can use **Switch organisation** to re-point the connection. QuickBooks connects a single company. If one provider is already connected, the app blocks the second: *"Xero is already connected. Disconnect it before connecting QuickBooks."* **Disconnect** pauses syncing and leaves all existing data in place.
</Aside>

## What syncs

Four flows ride the same connection — two pushing documents out to your provider, two pulling records in.

<div class="po-annot">
  <div class="arow"><span class="aidx">↑</span><div><div class="at">Sales invoices → your provider</div><div class="ad">When a sales order ships, Ashicore can push an invoice. Send it from the order with <span class="po-uikey">Send invoice to Xero</span> (or QuickBooks), or turn on the <span class="po-uikey">Sales invoices</span> automation to push automatically on shipment. The automatic-on-ship toggle is a Xero feature; on QuickBooks, push manually — and note taxable orders can't post to QuickBooks, which has no tax mapping. A failed push never blocks the shipment and is safe to retry.</div></div></div>
  <div class="arow"><span class="aidx">↑</span><div><div class="at">Supplier bills ← purchase orders</div><div class="ad">Create a payable bill from a purchase order — a manual step, never an automatic side effect of receiving. On Xero, a multi-vendor order splits into one bill group per supplier; QuickBooks creates a single bill. See <a href="/docs/purchasing/sync-a-bill-to-xero">Sync a supplier bill</a>.</div></div></div>
  <div class="arow"><span class="aidx">↓</span><div><div class="at">Purchase orders → for receiving</div><div class="ad">Import <strong>open</strong> purchase orders raised in your provider so you can receive them in Ashicore. A bulk import can create missing materials once you've reviewed them; a scheduled <span class="po-uikey">Purchase order import</span> skips any order that would create a new material, leaving it for you to import by hand.</div></div></div>
  <div class="arow"><span class="aidx">↓</span><div><div class="at">Customers &amp; suppliers ← Xero contacts</div><div class="ad">Pull existing contacts from Xero into Ashicore customers and suppliers, so you're not retyping your address book. Each run reports how many were created, updated, and skipped — available in the Xero connection only.</div></div></div>
</div>

## Document statuses

A supplier bill tracks two independent things: whether Ashicore successfully sent it, and where it sits in your billing workflow.

<div class="po-enums">
  <span class="po-enum">Creating bill</span>
  <span class="po-enum" style="color:var(--st-success-ink)">Billed</span>
  <span class="po-enum" style="color:var(--st-danger-ink)">Bill failed</span>
  <span class="po-enum">Not billed</span>
  <span class="po-enum">Partly billed</span>
  <span class="po-enum">Billed</span>
</div>

While a push runs the bill shows **Creating bill**, then settles to **Billed** or **Bill failed** (safe to retry). Separately, you can set a **manual bill status** — **Not billed**, **Partly billed**, or **Billed** — as the supplier invoice gets paid down. Creating a bill never changes a purchase order's status, and receiving never creates a bill, so billing and receiving stay independent.

## Posting defaults &amp; automation

Set the accounts and tax treatment Ashicore uses when it posts, from <span class="po-uikey">Defaults</span> on the provider card. These apply to new exports only.

| Setting | Applies to | What it controls |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Sales invoice account** | Invoices out | The income account new sales invoices post against. |
| **Purchase bill account** | Bills out | The default account for bills created from purchase orders. |
| **Tax treatment** | Invoices out · Xero | The output tax code on invoices — Output tax, Output tax reduced, Zero rated, Exempt, or No tax. (QuickBooks has no tax mapping; taxable orders can't post.) |
| **Send as** | Invoices out · Xero | Whether sales invoices arrive as **Draft** (review before approval) or **Approved**. |
| **Sales invoices** | Automation | Push invoices automatically the moment a sales order ships. (Xero only — on QuickBooks, push manually.) |
| **Purchase order import** | Automation | Import open purchase orders from your provider on a schedule. |

<Aside type="note" title="Xero and QuickBooks differ on defaults">
The account fields apply to both providers. **Tax treatment** and **Send as** are Xero invoice options — QuickBooks invoices are always posted as drafts for you to review there, and taxable orders can't post to QuickBooks.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. From <span class="po-uikey">Settings → Integrations</span>, choose **Connect** on Xero or QuickBooks and authorize the organisation in the provider's window.

2. Set your **defaults** — at minimum the sales invoice account and purchase bill account — so the first export posts where you expect.

3. Decide your **automation**: push invoices on shipment, and whether to import open purchase orders on a schedule.

4. Optionally run a one-time **import** of customers and suppliers to seed your address book.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="After disconnecting">
Existing data stays put, but new exports and imports pause until the provider is reconnected. Use <span class="po-uikey">Sync history</span> on the Xero card to review every push and import — each is recorded as **Success** or **Failed** with a short reason.
</Aside>
