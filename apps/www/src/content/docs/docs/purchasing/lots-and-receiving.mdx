Receiving is where a purchase order becomes real inventory. Each received line writes a permanent receipt. For lot-tracked items, that receipt creates a costed lot; for untracked items, it appends to the item's internal shared lot. Understanding that history explains the order's costing and traceability — and what happens to its stock if the order is later deleted.

## A lot is a received batch

When you receive a tracked item, Ashicore creates **one lot** holding the received stocking quantity, plus a permanent **receipt** referencing the order. Untracked items append to one internal shared lot instead. Receipts always land as **available**; block tracked stock from its lot action menu, or untracked stock with **Stock action** on the item card. A tracked lot's **disposition** is:

- **<Pill status="received" label="available" />** — Available stock, ready to consume or ship. This is how all receipts arrive.
- **<Pill status="draft" label="blocked" />** — held out of use: on hand but withheld from use until released. Set on a lot after receipt via inventory.

## Tracked vs. untracked items

| Mode | On receipt | After-receipt revaluation |
|------|-----------|---------------------------|
| <span class="po-enum">tracked</span> | Gets its own individual lot you can trace and disposition | By-value cost edits rebase the lot per location |
| <span class="po-enum">untracked</span> | Appends to a single shared moving-average bucket; can only be received as available | Saved but not rebased in v1 |

## Why receipts are permanent

A receipt is real inventory history — the events that put stock on your shelves stay intact, which is why received lines can't be removed. Deleting a received order doesn't erase them either: it removes the order's received stock that is still on hand and keeps used quantities and their cost history, so the record of what was received, and at what cost, survives the delete.

<Aside type="caution" title="Receiving is positive-only">
You can receive another partial batch while an order is <Pill status="partial" />, but you cannot receive directly against a fully <Pill status="received" /> order. Add a line or increase a quantity first to reopen a remainder, then receive the new quantity. There is no negative receipt; to back out received stock, delete the order — see [the lifecycle](/docs/purchasing/the-po-lifecycle#what-happens-when-you-delete-an-order).
</Aside>
