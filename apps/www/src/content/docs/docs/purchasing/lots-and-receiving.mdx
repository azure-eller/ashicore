Receiving is where a purchase order becomes real inventory. Each received line writes a permanent receipt. For lot-tracked items, that receipt creates a costed lot; for untracked items, it appends to the item's internal shared lot. Understanding that history explains the order's costing, traceability, and why it can't be deleted afterward.

## A lot is a received batch

When you receive a tracked item, Ashicore creates **one lot** holding the received stocking quantity, plus a permanent **receipt** referencing the order. Untracked items append to one internal shared lot instead. Receipts always land as **available**; a lot can later be moved to **blocked** from the inventory lot actions. The lot's **disposition** is:

- **<Pill status="received" label="available" />** — usable stock, ready to consume or ship. This is how all receipts arrive.
- **<Pill status="draft" label="blocked" />** — quarantined: on hand but withheld from use until released. Set on a lot after receipt via inventory.

## Tracked vs. untracked items

| Mode | On receipt | After-receipt revaluation |
|------|-----------|---------------------------|
| <span class="po-enum">tracked</span> | Gets its own individual lot you can trace and disposition | By-value cost edits rebase the lot per location |
| <span class="po-enum">untracked</span> | Appends to a single shared moving-average bucket; can only be received as available | Saved but not rebased in v1 |

## Why receipts are permanent

A receipt is real inventory history. That's the reason a partially- or fully-received order can no longer be deleted and received lines can't be removed — the events that put stock on your shelves must stay intact. To reverse received stock, adjust it through inventory; the order remains the record of what was received and at what cost.

<Aside type="caution" title="Receiving is positive-only">
You can receive another partial batch while an order is <Pill status="partial" />, but you cannot receive directly against a fully <Pill status="received" /> order. Add a line or increase a quantity first to reopen a remainder, then receive the new quantity. There is no negative receipt; see [the lifecycle](/docs/purchasing/the-po-lifecycle#why-cant-i-delete-this-order).
</Aside>
