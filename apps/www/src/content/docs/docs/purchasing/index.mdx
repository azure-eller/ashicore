## Purchase order statuses

An order has one status at a time. The status decides what you can edit, receive, or delete.

| Status | What it means | Edit | Receive | Delete |
|--------|---------------|------|---------|--------|
| <Pill status="draft" /> | Being built. Not counted toward expected supply. | <span class="tbl-can good">yes</span> | <span class="tbl-can">—</span> | <span class="tbl-can good">yes</span> |
| <Pill status="ordered" /> | Ordered from the supplier. Adds expected supply. | <span class="tbl-can good">yes</span> | <span class="tbl-can good">yes</span> | <span class="tbl-can good">before receipt</span> |
| <Pill status="partial" /> | Some lines received into inventory. | <span class="tbl-can good">yes</span> | <span class="tbl-can good">yes</span> | <span class="tbl-can no">blocked</span> |
| <Pill status="received" /> | Every line received. Editing up reverts to Partial. | <span class="tbl-can good">yes</span> | <span class="tbl-can">—</span> | <span class="tbl-can no">blocked</span> |
