Build a purchase order for the materials you buy from one supplier and set quantities in your purchase units. The first valid save creates an <Pill status="not_received" /> order and books expected supply.

<p class="po-meta">
  <span class="po-chip">6 steps · ~4 min</span>
  <span class="po-chip">Applies to: <b>New → Not received</b></span>
  <span class="po-chip">Access: <b>Purchasing operate</b></span>
</p>

<Aside type="note" title="Before you start">
You'll need at least one **supplier** and active items to buy. Items can carry a purchase unit and purchase-to-stock conversion; when they do not, the line falls back to the stocking unit with a factor of `1`. You also need **operate** access to Purchasing (owners and admins have it by default; team members need it assigned).
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Start a new order.** From **Purchasing > Orders**, choose **New Purchase Order**. Ashicore opens an unsaved card titled **New purchase order**. You can enter a purchase order number right away, or leave it blank so Ashicore assigns the next PO number on the first successful save. Once the card first saves with a supplier and line, Ashicore changes the URL to the saved order and shows it as <Pill status="not_received" />.

   <p class="mk-cap">The purchase orders list. New orders open unsaved; after a valid autosave, you can leave and return to them at any time.</p>

2. **Choose a supplier.** Pick who you're buying from. Both a supplier and one complete material line are required before the first save; until then, the save indicator says **Supplier is required** or **Add a material to save**. Your edits stay on the unsaved card. The supplier's name is copied onto the order when the order is saved. Later edits to the purchase order refresh that copied name from the current supplier record.

       </div>
     </div>
   </Frame>

3. **Add material lines.** Choose **Add material** for each item you're ordering. Pick the item, enter **Quantity** in its **UoM** (for example, a 69 kg bag), and review **Price per unit**. The price prefills from the item's default purchase price (and its default tax rate) and stays editable. Ashicore converts to stocking units and can show the resulting **Landed cost** — the stock-unit cost your inventory is valued at.

   <Aside type="tip" title="Purchase unit vs. stock unit">
   A "Bag (×69 kg)" line ordered twelve times becomes 828 kg of inventory. The **landed cost per stock unit** climbs above the raw `$310 ÷ 69 = $4.49` once by-value additional costs are allocated in the next step.
   </Aside>

4. **Add additional costs (optional).** Choose **Add cost** to capture freight, customs, or other charges. In the **Cost** column, pick **Shipping**, **Customs**, or **Other**. Amount is required for any cost row you begin, including one where you only choose a separate supplier. For each row, choose how it's distributed: **By value** uses each line's share of material value, **By quantity** uses its share of ordered purchase units, and **Not distributed** raises the order total without changing inventory valuation.

   <Aside type="caution" title="Where the money lands">
   **By value** and **By quantity** costs flow into your inventory cost and, for tracked items, the lot cost when you receive. A **Not distributed** cost raises the order total but never the inventory valuation. Editing a distributed cost *after* receipt revalues tracked stock still on hand from this order; untracked shared-bucket materials save the edit without rebasing in v1.
   </Aside>

5. **Set delivery details.** Add the delivery address and an optional expected arrival date. There is no Save button: valid changes autosave. Until you choose a supplier and add one complete material line, the card remains unsaved; the save indicator names the missing requirement. After the first valid save, it has a PO number, is counted in expected supply, and can be reopened. It stays editable and deletable after receipt; deleting received orders removes stock still on hand and keeps used quantities in history.

6. **Send it.** The normal supplier-facing path is the header mail action: choose **Send PO email**, review **Send documents for PO-1045**, and send the email. The order is already <Pill status="not_received" /> and expected supply is already live; email only sends the supplier document.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Done — the order is Not received">
Expected supply is live after the first valid save, and the order remains editable and deletable throughout its lifecycle. Next, when stock arrives, **[receive it into inventory](/docs/purchasing/receive-stock)**.
</Aside>
