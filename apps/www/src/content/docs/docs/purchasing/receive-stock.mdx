Turn ordered quantities into real, costed inventory. Receiving is driven from the order's status menu: choose **Received** to prefill all remaining quantities, or **Partially received** to start with blank quantities and fill only what arrived. Each line you receive writes permanent receipt history; lot-tracked items get a new lot.

<p class="po-meta">
  <span class="po-chip">3 steps · ~3 min</span>
  <span class="po-chip">Applies to: <b>Not received / Partially received</b></span>
  <span class="po-chip">Access: <b>Purchasing operate</b></span>
</p>

<Aside type="note" title="Receiving starts from the status menu">
Only <Pill status="not_received" /> or <Pill status="partial" /> orders can be received. Picking **Received** or **Partially received** opens the receive dialog — it doesn't change the status until you confirm.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Open the receive dialog from the status menu.** On a not received or partially received order, open the **status** menu and choose **Received** (prefill all remaining quantities) or **Partially received** (start blank). Both open the **Receive purchase order** dialog — "Enter the quantities received for each material."

     </div>

2. **Enter quantities and the receiving location.** The dialog lists every line with its **Ordered**, **Received**, and **Remaining** quantities, plus a **Receive now** input (in purchase units). Choosing *Received* prefills each row to its remaining amount; leave those values unchanged to complete the order, or reduce/clear rows to receive less. Choosing *Partially received* starts blank so you fill only what arrived. Pick the **Receive into** location — leave it as default unless stock landed elsewhere.

   <Aside type="note" title="You can't receive more than remaining">
   "Receive now" is capped at each line's remaining quantity — a larger number is flagged invalid and the dialog won't submit. Leave a line blank (or 0) to skip it; that's a partial receipt. Received stock lands as **available**; block it afterward from the lot action menu for tracked stock or **Stock action** on an untracked item card.
   </Aside>

3. **Confirm — stock, lots, and status update.** Choose **Mark received** when you left the full prefill in place, or **Receive selected** for the lines you filled. Each received line writes a permanent receipt at the landed stock-unit cost; tracked items get a new lot, while untracked items append to their internal shared lot. The matching expected supply is released, and the status becomes <Pill status="partial" /> (lines remain) or <Pill status="received" /> (all complete).

   <Aside type="caution" title="Receiving is positive-only">
   You can receive another partial batch while the order is <Pill status="partial" />, but you can't "un-receive." Once the order is fully <Pill status="received" />, add a line or increase a quantity first to reopen a remainder, then receive the new quantity. Received lines can't be removed because receipt history must be preserved. To back out the whole order, delete it — stock still on hand is removed and used quantities keep their history ([details](/docs/purchasing/the-po-lifecycle#what-happens-when-you-delete-an-order)).
   </Aside>

</Steps>
