Use variants when one product or material has several real sellable or stockable forms. The family groups the shared card details, while each variant remains a concrete item row with its own SKU, price, stock, lots, and ledger history.

## The family shape

<div class="po-annot">
  <div class="arow"><span class="aidx">1</span><div><div class="at">The family card</div><div class="ad">The family card holds the shared identity: name, item type, category, description, stocking unit, lot-tracking mode, and shared purchasing setup. Editing these fields on the card mirrors the change to every variant item.</div></div></div>
  <div class="arow"><span class="aidx">2</span><div><div class="at">Options and values</div><div class="ad">Options are the axes of variation — for example, Bag size with values 0.5 ft3, 1 ft3, 2 ft3. Values are ordered and unique within their option. The card UI supports up to 3 option axes.</div></div></div>
  <div class="arow"><span class="aidx">3</span><div><div class="at">Variant items</div><div class="ad">Each option-value combination produces one stockable item row with its own SKU, price, stock, lots, and ledger history. Every combination in the family must be unique.</div></div></div>
</div>

## Rules

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Shared fields are edited on the family card and mirror to all variants. | Name, item type, category, unit, lot-tracking mode, and purchasing setup are managed once. |
| Per-variant fields are independent. | SKU, barcodes, supplier code, lead time, MOQ, prices, stock quantity, and sellability belong to each concrete variant item. |
| Each option-value combination must be unique. | The API exposes duplicate-combination warnings; filled duplicate SKUs are hard-blocked. |
| The card UI supports up to 3 option axes. | Each axis holds an ordered list of values. At least one active variant must remain on the card. |

## Pricing and downstream workflows

A sales pricing schedule can target a variant option and value, such as every 2 ft3 bag. Downstream workflows still use the concrete item row: purchase orders, sales lines, lots, stocktakes, and ledger events move stock on the variant, not on the family record.

<Aside type="note" title="A variant is a real item">
Use the family to organize the range. Use the concrete variant whenever the operator needs stock, cost, price, demand, expected supply, lots, or ledger history.
</Aside>
