Stock is projected from inventory events. Lots carry quantity, disposition, location, and cost, so the app can preserve audit history while showing current balances.

<p class="po-meta">
  <span class="po-chip">ledger-backed</span>
  <span class="po-chip"><b>3</b> dispositions</span>
  <span class="po-chip">default location aware</span>
</p>

## Dispositions

| Disposition | Meaning | Counts toward usable stock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Available | Ready to sell, consume, or allocate. | yes |
| Blocked | Held for review or quality control. | no |
| Rejected | Failed quality review and may be scrapped. | no |

Only available stock is treated as consumable or sellable. Blocking and rejection preserve the lot trail instead of hiding the quantity.
