Customers are the accounts you sell to. Sales orders snapshot customer details so old orders keep their historical context even when the customer record changes.

<p class="po-meta">
  <span class="po-chip">soft delete</span>
  <span class="po-chip">contact roles</span>
  <span class="po-chip">pricing category</span>
</p>

## Account state

| State | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| Active | The account is current and ordering normally. |
| Growth | The account needs attention because there is expansion potential. |
| At risk | The account needs follow-up or recovery work. |
| Former | The account is retained for history but no longer active. |
