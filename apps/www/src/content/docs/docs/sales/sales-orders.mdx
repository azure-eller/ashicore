Sales orders store the customer commitment. The database stores open or done, while the UI derives delivery state from shipped and cancelled quantities.

## Delivery states

| State | What it means | Edit | Deliver | Delete |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Not shipped | No line quantities have shipped. | yes | yes | yes |
| Partially shipped | Some, but not all, ordered quantity has shipped. | limited | yes | no |
| Shipped | Every deliverable line has shipped or been cancelled after partial delivery. | no | no | no |

## Print sales documents

Open a saved order's header menu to print the sales order, include line
statuses, omit discounts, or produce a packing list with or without lot-tracing
details. Packing lists show remaining quantities while an order is open and shipped
quantities after completion. Planned lot tracing is open-order pick guidance; completed
orders offer the standard packing list because shipment-history tracing is not yet
available. **Print** opens the complete server-rendered PDF in a new tab, while
**Download sales order PDF** saves the standard order. From the orders list,
select up to 50 orders to print or download the standard orders together as one
PDF.
