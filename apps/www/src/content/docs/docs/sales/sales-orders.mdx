Sales orders store the customer commitment. The database stores open or done, while the UI derives delivery state from shipped quantities.

<p class="po-meta">
  <span class="po-chip"><b>3</b> delivery states</span>
  <span class="po-chip">pricing resolves on line entry</span>
  <span class="po-chip">shipped lines lock</span>
</p>

## Delivery states

| State | What it means | Edit | Deliver | Delete |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Not shipped | No line quantities have shipped. | yes | yes | yes |
| Partially shipped | Some, but not all, ordered quantity has shipped. | limited | yes | no |
| Shipped | Every deliverable line has shipped. | no | no | no |
