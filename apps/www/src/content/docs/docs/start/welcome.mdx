Ashicore is an inventory-first operations app — a light MRP for small-scale batch manufacturers who buy materials, make products to a recipe, and ship them to customers. One lot-backed, costed inventory core sits at the center, and Purchasing, Manufacturing, and Sales all read from and write to it. This page orients you before you dive into a module.

## The model

Ashicore is event-sourced at its core. Every movement — a receipt, a build, a shipment, an adjustment — is recorded as an event in an append-only inventory ledger. On-hand quantity, demand, expected supply, and available-to-promise are all projected from that ledger, never typed in by hand. The four modules are different lenses onto the same shared core: Purchasing writes stock in, Manufacturing turns materials into products, Sales commits and ships them out, and Stocktakes reconciles the records to what is physically on the shelf.

<Aside type="note" title="Read the ledger concept early">
Most "why is this number what it is?" questions resolve once the ledger clicks. See [The inventory ledger](/docs/inventory/ledger) and [On-hand, demand &amp; ATP](/docs/inventory/on-hand-demand-and-atp).
</Aside>

## Good to know

- **Built for batch makers.** Materials, products defined by a bill of materials, and production in discrete or batch runs.
- **Lot- and cost-aware throughout.** Stock is tracked in lots with cost attached; margin on a sale is estimated up front and booked for real when it ships.
- **Not a general ledger.** Ashicore syncs accounting documents to Xero or QuickBooks rather than keeping its own AP/AR, GL, or payments — your accounting system stays the book of record.
- **No capacity or auto-MRP planning.** You plan and prioritize manufacturing orders directly; there's no work-center scheduling or auto-generated MRP run.
