Open manufacturing orders have a unique positive priority rank. The rank controls queue order; completing or deleting an order clears its rank and reranks the remaining open orders.

<p class="po-meta">
  <span class="po-chip">2 steps · ~2 min</span>
  <span class="po-chip">Applies to: <b>Open orders</b></span>
  <span class="po-chip">Access: <b>Manufacturing operate</b></span>
</p>

<Steps>

1. **Drag to reorder.** Open **Manufacturing > Manufacturing orders** on the **Open** tab. When the list has no search, resource filter, or sort override, drag the **Rank** column to reorder open orders. Ashicore renumbers the submitted queue as `1..n`.

   <Frame title="Ashicore · <b>Open manufacturing orders</b>">
     <Grid>
       <thead><tr><th>Rank</th><th>Order</th><th>Product</th><th>Production</th><th>Deadline</th></tr></thead>
       <tbody>
         <tr><td class="num">1</td><td><span class="num">MO-2052</span></td><td>Premium Potting Mix</td><td><Pill status="partial" label="Work in progress" /></td><td>Apr 24</td></tr>
         <tr><td class="num">2</td><td><span class="num">MO-2053</span></td><td>Raised-Bed Blend</td><td><Pill status="draft" label="Not started" /></td><td>Apr 25</td></tr>
         <tr><td class="num">3</td><td><span class="num">MO-2054</span></td><td>Screened Compost</td><td><Pill status="ordered" label="Blocked" /></td><td>Apr 26</td></tr>
       </tbody>
     </Grid>
   </Frame>

2. **Filter, then focus.** Use search and the resource filter to find work, inspect ingredient coverage, or compare deadlines. Clear filters before dragging the queue again. A single order can also be assigned a rank, but duplicate active ranks are rejected.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Rank is only for open work">
Done orders do not keep a queue rank. If two users try to assign the same rank, Ashicore returns: `Priority rank N is already assigned to another active order.`
</Aside>
