Bills of materials are managed from product cards, not from a separate Manufacturing BOM screen. Use the product Recipe tab for components and lot-age constraints, and the Production tab for operation costs and resources.

<p class="po-meta">
  <span class="po-chip">product card</span>
  <span class="po-chip">revisioned</span>
  <span class="po-chip">snapshotted by orders</span>
</p>

## What a BOM contains

| Part | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Components | Required item variants and positive quantities per recipe basis. A product cannot include itself as a component, and manufacturing orders can swap a component only to another active variant in the same item family. |
| Constraints | Lot strategy and minimum lot-age requirements in positive whole days. |
| Operation costs | Resources, crew, minutes, and rate inputs for cost rollup. |
