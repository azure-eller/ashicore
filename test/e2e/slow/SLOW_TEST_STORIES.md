# Slow Test Stories

Only these slow spec files are allowed. Slow specs are operating stories, not
bug archives: each file must describe a workflow a small manufacturer would
recognize and must avoid incidental UI/regression breadth.

| File | Lane | Active tests | Story | Must prove | Not covered | Safety | Justification |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sales-fulfillment.spec.ts` | `sales` | 8 | A customer places an order directly or through Ash approval, demand appears, activities are tagged to a project and filtered by chip, a follow-up task is logged and worked off the due queue, stock ships partially/finally, pricing suggestions resolve, and order/inventory state stays correct. | Ash stages without committing until approval, approved draft edits persist, customer context snapshot, project-tagged activity + chip/pill stream filtering, task create + due-queue surfacing + completion (`done` + `completedAt`), demand creation, partial/final shipment, order status, stock consumed once, delete releases demand, pricing scope precedence, quantity break boundaries, manual price override preservation | CRM breadth, full pricing matrix, card/action UI beyond the approval seam, delete guard matrix | `serial-only` | Order fulfillment and pricing-scenario modeling are different operator workflows on different documents; folding the scenario arc here would bloat an already 8-test story. |
| `purchasing-receiving.spec.ts` | `purchasing` | 10 | A buyer raises a PO directly or through Ash approval, sends supplier documents, receives it in parts, expected supply becomes physical stock, and later corrects freight and the invoiced line price: eligible available tracked stock revalues landed cost via append-only events, while consumed and untracked-v1 stock do not block the PO edit. | Ash stages a PO without committing until approval (a non-sales registry action through the same stage seam), supplier/PO setup, creation-time expected supply, sent supplier-copy state in the email sheet, partial/final receipt, lot/balance truth, freight-edit landed-cost revaluation (eligible lot + material cost rebased, receipt untouched), freight save after consumption with remaining available revaluation, received-card price edit through the editable price cell revaluing remaining stock, received-card quantity cell remains editable, untracked v1 freight save without revaluation | all supplier fields, validation variants, delete receipt-reversal DB effects (fast lane covers them; the story only peeks at the delete dialog copy), untracked MAC revaluation (phase 2), card/action UI beyond the approval seam | `serial-only` | The Ash approval test is the one non-sales registry action protecting the generic stage→approve→commit seam outside the sales lane; folding it here avoids a new slow spec. |
| `manufacturing-execution.spec.ts` | `manufacturing` | 7 | An operator reviews unit, batch, and operation-only recipe cost previews, tests draft quantity and ingredient changes without saving, then releases, picks, and completes an MO with correct ingredient and output stock. | Current cost for saved and newly selected components, saved batch revision output basis, operation-only product cost, live draft contribution and product total, unsaved draft isolation, BOM snapshot, ingredient demand, pick consumption, output stock, done status, compact output cost truth, delete rolls back expected output | unresolved-cost permutations, recipe-history costing, responsive layout details, replay/concurrency/idempotency matrix, negative-stock override branches, cost-basis permutations | `serial-only` | |

| `pricing-scenarios-story.spec.ts` | `sales` | 2 | An owner models product pricing from live recipe costs, overrides a material price, commits a revision as the decision record, watches ERP prices move without disturbing that record, then clears the override and commits the new reality as revision 2; and a fresh scenario pre-fills its overhead from the org's Xero-derived rate, shows that provenance, and opens the worksheet inline. | live-baseline recalculation while typing, autosave without a save button, shared override propagation, commit freezing inputs and results, ERP drift moving the live baseline but never a committed snapshot, revision history ordering, a new scenario's overhead field pre-filling from the saved org overhead default, Xero provenance, inline worksheet doorway | picker breadth, duplicate/delete UI, conflict-recovery matrix (fast lane covers the 409 seam), the Xero P&L fetch itself (compute + persistence live in the fast lane) | `serial-only` | Pricing scenarios are a separate operator workflow (costing decisions over live recipes with frozen revision history), not an order-fulfillment step; it cannot fold into sales-fulfillment without mixing unrelated stories. The overhead default and its worksheet are part of the pricing decision, so they belong with the scenario story. |
| `stocktake-workflow.spec.ts` | `stocktake` | 12 | An operator creates a stocktake, saves/reloads sparse counts, must supply a reason to commit, sees reconciled state carrying that reason, and records an unexpected found lot that becomes new lot stock with a posted gain. Found lots reusing an existing lot number are resolved to the canonical lot, live lots created after the snapshot require stale confirmation before reconciling to the count, zero-balance historical lots are reused, partial lot counts preserve untouched lot totals, re-saving the same found lot is idempotent, and the unified reconciliation route rejects inline stocktake lines. | draft persistence, reload/continue, zero-lot tracked rows can open found-lot entry, blank-reason completion rejected (400), stocktake reconciliation rejects inline lines, commit with a reason persisted onto the stocktake row and stocktake_gain companion reason row, completed reconciliation state, found-lot creation + stocktake_gain to the new lot, found-lot canonicalization when the lot number already exists for the item, stale confirmation for a live lot created after the stocktake snapshot, zero-balance historical lot reuse, partial lot completion rollup, idempotent found-lot save (no duplicate rows on retry) | basic count-to-event math already covered by fast, category variants, clone UI action | `serial-only` | Stocktake is one serial reconciliation story; keeping lot discovery, zero-balance visibility, partial-lot completion, and idempotent retry together avoids another slow spec while protecting the operator workflow. |
